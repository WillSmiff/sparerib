Welcome to the Official SBA Homepage!!

Howdy and welcome, fellow pin-pushers! The Sparerib Bowling Association has been bringing together bowlers of every age and skill level for nearly 40 years. Whether you're chasing a perfect 300 or just hoping to stay out of the gutter, there's a lane for you here.

We run 6 weekly leagues, 4 sanctioned tournaments a year, and the famous Sparerib Rib-Off™ — where every strike in the 10th frame earns you a free rack of ribs from the snack bar. (Limit one rack per bowler. Seriously, Gary.)
